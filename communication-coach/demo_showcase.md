- ✅ **TailwindCSS**: Modern, responsive design
- ✅ **Framer Motion**: Smooth animations
- ✅ **Component Architecture**: Modular, reusable components

### **Backend (FastAPI + Python)**
- ✅ **RESTful API**: Complete endpoint structure
- ✅ **Audio Analysis**: Emotion classification, WPM, filler words
- ✅ **Landmark Processing**: Posture, eye contact, smile detection
- ✅ **Session Management**: History, reports, export
- ✅ **Mock Responses**: Ready for immediate testing

### **AI/ML Features**
- ✅ **Heuristics Engine**: Rule-based analysis algorithms
- ✅ **Real-time Processing**: <200ms latency for feedback

- ✅ **Multi-modal**: Audio + video analysis integration
